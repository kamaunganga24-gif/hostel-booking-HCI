User Research Report: Hostel Booking System

 1. Project Background & Problem Statement
Finding suitable university hostel accommodation is a major challenge for tertiary students. Existing systems rely on physical notice boards, scattered social media groups, or outdated university portals that lack real-time availability updates.

 Key Pain Points Identified:
Information Fragmentation: Room details, amenities, and pricing are scattered across multiple non-standardized platforms.
Lack of Transparency:** Students cannot easily compare two or more hostels side-by-side to make informed decisions.
Complex Application & Payment Procedures:** Multi-step paper or clunky digital forms often result in lost application statuses and missing deposit tracking.


 2. Target User Personas

 Persona A: The Incoming Freshman
Name: Agnes Wanjiku (Age 18)
Goal: Wants to secure a safe, budget-friendly single room near campus before arriving for orientation.
Frustration: Overwhelmed by long, complicated registration forms and hesitant to pay deposits without immediate confirmation.

### Persona B: The Returning Student
Name:David Nganga (Age 21)
Goal: Needs to quickly compare 2–3 private hostels based on specific amenities (e.g., high-speed Wi-Fi, laundry facilities).
Frustration: Waste time visiting multiple websites that do not display real-time room availability.


3. Key Usability Requirements (HCI Focus)
1. Simplified Search & Filter: Users must be able to filter by budget (ksh/month), proximity, and room type within 2 clicks.
2. Direct Comparison Tool: Users need a dedicated side-by-side comparison screen highlighting key feature differences.
3. Progressive Form Disclosure: Multi-field application forms must be broken into clear steps (e.g., Step 1: Personal Info, Step 2: Documents).
4. Immediate Feedback & Proof: Every transaction (e.g., deposit payment) must trigger a clear success state with a digital receipt.
